## Honest bar

| Level | Coding | Systems / platform | Behavioral |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SDET II | easy + medium basics | framework + API/SQL | 5 good stories |
| Senior SDET | medium fluency | framework + CI + debugging + some design | 6–8 stories |
| SDET III | medium + selected advanced | concurrency + distributed + test platform + HLD/LLD | 8–10 stories |
| Lead SDET | medium enough to stay credible | strategy + platform + roadmap + metrics | org-wide leadership stories |

## Amazon focus

- coding under time matters more than many candidates expect
- leadership principles follow-ups are deep
- framework/platform scalability matters
- trade-offs and metrics matter more than tool trivia

## Apple focus

- quality as product and release confidence
- detail orientation and debugging depth
- cross-functional collaboration and craft
- mobile/platform awareness if the role is ecosystem-specific

## Final readiness check

You are close to loop-ready when you can do all of these without notes:

- solve medium coding problems cleanly in Java
- explain one automation framework architecture end to end
- design a distributed test platform on a whiteboard
- debug a flaky or distributed failure with structured reasoning
- tell 8–10 metric-backed stories mapped to company style
- discuss security, observability, and quality strategy like an engineering owner
