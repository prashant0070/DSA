# Lead SDET — interview Q&A

**Notes:** [NOTES.md](NOTES.md)

### Q1. How would you spend first 90 days as Lead SDET?
Listen + measure baseline (flake, CI, escapes) → quick win on trust → draft roadmap with stakeholders → pilot one paved path → socialize metrics.

### Q2. Two frameworks across teams — your call?
Assess overlap/cost/bus factor. Options: shared core, gradual converge, federated with governance (reporting schema, quality gates). Pilot data beats mandate.

### Q3. How influence without authority?
Data, make developers faster, willing pilot squad, exec sponsor for gates, public wins.

### Q4. Quality blocking releases — plan?
Stop bleed, quarantine, restore smoke, weekly exec trend, RCA owners, temporary risk accept with expiry.

### Q5. What do you hire for in Senior SDET?
Medium coding, debugging, system sense, communication, ownership — not tool trivia.

### Q6. Tell me a Lead-level story structure.
Org problem → coalition → technical plan → resistance → metric outcome → what you’d repeat differently.
