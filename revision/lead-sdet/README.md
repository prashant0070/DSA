# Lead SDET track

**Notes:** [NOTES.md](NOTES.md)  
**Interview Q&A:** [INTERVIEW-QA.md](INTERVIEW-QA.md)

Lead SDET is not just “senior with more Selenium.”

It is technical ownership of quality outcomes across teams.

## Scope

### Technical leadership

- quality strategy
- framework and platform direction
- architecture reviews
- build vs buy decisions
- release confidence and risk language

### Organizational leadership

- mentoring and hiring
- influencing teams without authority
- cross-functional alignment with PM, dev, design, release, security
- driving standards without bureaucracy

### Metrics and business impact

- regression time reduction
- flake reduction
- release frequency impact
- escaped defects
- team productivity and trust

### Interview expectations

- platform roadmap
- conflict handling
- quality investment trade-offs
- communicating with directors and VPs
- 12-month improvement plan
